How are these estimates made?

Water use is estimated as reference evapotranspiration (calculated from the forecast temperatures with the Hargreaves-Samani method) multiplied by a crop coefficient for the crop's growth stage. Rain under 2 mm is ignored and 80% of larger rain is counted. A simple soil-water store (assumed to hold 12 mm now and at most 25 mm) shows when it runs dry.

Fertilizer guidance shows timing and the nutrients to focus on, never doses. Doses depend on your soil test, variety and local conditions.
